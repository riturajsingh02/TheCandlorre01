We offer FREE Express Pan-India Shipping on all orders of ₹999 and above. For orders below ₹999, a nominal flat shipping fee of ₹99 is applied at checkout to cover insured courier transit.

5 Candle Care & Burn Rituals

What is the "Memory Burn" and why is it crucial?

On your candle's very first lighting, allow it to burn continuously for 2 to 3 hours until the liquid wax pool reaches the outer glass rim. Soy wax has memory: if extinguished too soon, it will tunnel down the center, drastically shortening candle life.

Why should I trim the cotton wick before lighting?

Always trim the cotton wick to 1/4 inch (6mm) prior to every burn. A trimmed wick ensures an even flame, eliminates excess carbon mushrooming, and prevents black soot from adhering to the glass vessel.

How long should I burn the candle in a single session?

We recommend burning your candle for a maximum of 3 to 4 hours at a time. Burning beyond 4 hours can cause the vessel to overheat and consume fragrance oils too rapidly.

How do I fix a candle that has already started tunneling?

Wrap aluminum foil around the top rim of the candle like a dome, leaving a 1-inch opening at the center above the flame. Burn for 1–2 hours. The trapped heat will melt the hardened wax rim back into a uniform flat pool.

How do I clean and repurpose the jar after the wax finishes?

When approximately 1/2 inch of wax remains, discontinue burning. Pour hot (not boiling) tap water into the jar; the leftover soy wax will soften and float to the surface. Once cooled, pop the wax puck out, clean with warm soapy water, and repurpose the jar as a luxe makeup brush holder, pen stand, or succulent planter.

6 Reed Diffusers & Flame-Free Aromas

How do I set up and maintain a Reed Diffuser?

Carefully unseal the glass diffuser bottle and insert the natural fiber reed sticks into the scented oil. Allow the reeds to absorb oil for 2–3 hours, then flip the sticks to jumpstart aroma dispersion. For a burst of refreshed scent throw, flip the reeds once a week.

How long do your Reed Diffusers last?

Lifespan may vary based on air conditioning, ceiling fan proximity, and room temperature.

Where is the optimal location to place diffusers and candles?

Place diffusers in high-traffic transition areas such as foyers, hallways, vanity counters, powder rooms, and bedroom dressers. Keep them away from direct sunlight, air conditioner vents, and drafty windows to prevent rapid oil evaporation.

7 Ingredients, Wax & Safety Standards

What wax is used in The Candlorre candles?

We exclusively use 100% natural botanical soy wax blended with organic beeswax. We never use petroleum-derived paraffin, synthetic stearic acid, or chemical bleaching agents.

Are your candles and oils safe for pets and children?

Yes. All our aromatic formulations strictly conform to IFRA (International Fragrance Association) global standards and are 100% free of phthalates, parabens, and hazardous VOCs. For fire safety, always burn candles on a stable heat-proof surface out of reach of unattended pets and children.

Why do soy candles sometimes develop "frosting" or white crystals on top?

"Frosting" is a natural polymorphic crystal formation unique to 100% pure soy wax. It is an authentic hallmark of all-natural vegetable wax containing zero paraffin additives. It does not affect fragrance diffusion or burn performance in any way.
